Privacy Policy

Notice: This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling practices. We prioritize local execution and zero credential interception. Please review with your internal compliance officer.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website (https://rotrixo.com) or submit our contact form, we collect minimal information necessary to deliver our engineering consulting and software services:

  • Contact Information: Name, work email address, agency name, country, and inquiry specifications submitted voluntarily via our contact form.
  • Technical Metadata: Standard, non-personally identifiable server telemetry generated by Netlify (e.g. browser type, operating system, referring URL, timestamp) used for operational diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process submitted data exclusively for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Evaluating and responding to your custom automation engine inquiries.
  • Coordinating deployment sessions and communicating project updates.
  • Issuing Upwork Direct Contract escrow milestones and invoice records.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact data to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

3. Zero Key Interception & API Key Isolation

🔒 Critical Security Guarantee: Rotrixo does not maintain central servers that store, route, or proxy your Google Gemini, ElevenLabs, Apify, or Pixabay API keys.

All video generation pipelines operate entirely within your local execution environment or private Streamlit container. Your credentials reside in your private .env file on your own machine. When your engine runs, calls travel directly from your hardware to model providers using TLS encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

Our operations interact with trusted third-party service providers that maintain stringent data protection standards:

  • Netlify: Hosts our static web infrastructure and securely receives encrypted contact form submissions.
  • Upwork: Processes secure Direct Contract payments and escrow protection.
  • Google Gemini API: Foundation LLM API used directly by your engine for hook generation.
  • ElevenLabs: Audio AI model used directly by your engine for voice synthesis.
  • Apify & Pixabay: Scrapers and video APIs queried for trend benchmarking and stock footage.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Rotrixo does not use intrusive third-party cross-site advertising cookies (such as Facebook Pixel or programmatic ad retargeters). Minimal session storage may be used purely for essential user interface states (e.g. remembering navigation preferences).

6. Data Security & Retention

All web traffic across rotrixo.com is enforced via HTTPS using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. We retain submitted inquiry communications only for as long as necessary to fulfill business interactions, support client systems, or meet accounting obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & Global)

Regardless of your geographic location, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you.
  • Request immediate correction or permanent deletion of your contact records.
  • Withdraw consent for ongoing communication at any time.
